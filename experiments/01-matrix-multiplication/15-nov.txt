** 2. Wall time / why large cases stay incomplete

The multiplication work grows as n^3. Rough wall times on this machine (one multiply):
  n = 2048   about 2 to 5 s (depending on the version)
  n = 4096   about 20 to 40 s
  n = 8192   does not fit in the ~3 GB of this WSL2

So a full tile-size + layout + oblivious sweep at n = 4096 was expected to take more than an
hour and was stopped on 2026-11-14 (see 14-nov.txt, 7.4). Large tile sizes (T up to 512) were
already measured at n <= 2048; the bottleneck for "too long" is the matrix dimension n, not
the tile size. The same reason applies to finishing the multi-layout cache-counter run at
n = 2048 (each version is measured twice under perf, with 1 and 4 repetitions).
